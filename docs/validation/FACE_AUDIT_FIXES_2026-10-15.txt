BACKENBEBEN — FACE AUDIT CORRECTIONS — 2026-10-15

Scope
User requested all corrections from the v36 audit: beard mimic, upper teeth,
oral cavity, beard roots/material, cheek/ear refinement and weaker hairstyles.
These changes are in the editable Blender source and native game assets.
No AI image generation, beauty retouch, or third-party reference publication.
Arena/table/Hoenhorst and the original character-v3.blend remain unchanged.

Anatomical lip ownership
The audit exposed a limitation beyond the two original missing features:
reclassifying moved lips with one fixed Y equation was unstable across the
six identities, especially after making the neutral seam nearly closed.
The source now authors per-vertex jaw weights. Upper and lower oral rims
retain separate ownership, with restrained rounding at the commissures.
The same array is exported in face_customizable_collision.json:jaw_weights
and Face UV2.V. The glTF V-axis inversion is explicitly reversed by both
toon and outline shaders. Beard weights interpolate the same face triangles.
CPU contact deformation uses that exact exported array, not a second heuristic.
The source validator checks UV weights against the server array at every loop.
The deformation functions accept the per-vertex weight as an explicit argument;
there is no mutable shader global. Normal handling uses the same bound call.

Mouth topology and teeth
The neutral slit is reduced to an approximately 1.3-micrometre authoring gap
(source scale is multiplied by four in game). This avoids visible enamel
through a nominally closed mouth. Distinct jaw weights allow it to open
without depending on the tiny rest-space gap as a classification threshold.
Oral strip quads are split on the diagonal with the best forward projected
winding. All six final identity profiles pass the existing winding tolerance.
An intermediate micro-gap build failed this gate; tolerance was not relaxed.
Upper crowns are lowered into the aperture when the jaw opens. The lower
incisor row is raised to fit a modest overlapping bite behind the upper row.
Sixteen separate health/chip/missing indices and the real lower jaw hinge remain.

Interior
The old dark cylinder is replaced by a curved bowl, posterior closure,
upper/lower gum ridges, palate and a tongue form with distinct muted materials.
The surfaces deform with the existing jaw and permanent regional damage.
Commissure rounding avoids moving the central incisor span with an arbitrary
taper. This is a stylized interior, not a medical reconstruction or a complete
set of molars, throat anatomy and soft-tissue collision constraints.

Beard mimic and rendering
All four geometric beards now carry a nonempty grin shape transferred by
nearest-triangle barycentric interpolation of the actual face expression.
Original offsets are preserved rather than snapping moustache tips onto skin.
main.gd already drives grin during emotes on all matching mesh shapes.
CharacterView.set_grin drives the same parts for study captures and tests.
The updated captures no longer set only the Face morph.
UV2.U stores distance from each patch boundary. A rest-space coverage pattern
creates a sparse fringe without alpha sorting or camera-anchored noise.
Inverted-hull outlines are removed from beards; their own silhouette remains.
Shorter, finer fiber shading adds restrained variation instead of broad stripes.

Other sculpt and groom changes
Cheek fairing stays outside the oral loops and includes restrained tangential
relaxation to reduce uneven side-plane shading. Ear concha/antihelix relief
is modeled in the attached skin surface rather than added as detached pieces.
The shadow tint returns to a restrained cooler comic hue.
Side-part locks are sampled from the actual side-part scalp and receive a
dedicated direction of drawn strokes. Curls use smaller embedded, irregular,
flattened clumps with outward-facing surfaces, rather than large raised beads.
An intermediate high-density spiral experiment was rejected; the final curls
use compact meshes. Correct sphere winding also applies to the tongue.
These remain stylized options; matching the supplied XIII illustration is
subject to the user's visual acceptance, not established by automated tests.

New dental image regression
For each of six identities, render five otherwise identical states:
open healthy / open upper-eight missing / open lower-eight missing /
closed healthy / closed all missing.
Require at least the existing eight-enamel-pixel difference for EACH row.
Require at most two visibly changed mouth pixels between the closed images
(RGB difference > .03). Comparing the closed pair avoids treating neutral
skin highlights as enamel. This gate found real earlier penetrations and
was not relaxed to accept them.
