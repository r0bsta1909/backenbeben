Browser review method
tests/face_match_review.py opens two real clients for each combination:
Markant / Seitenscheitel / Schnurrbart / skin0;
Weich / Locken / Glattrasiert / skin5;
Veteran / Buzz Cut / Vollbart / skin2.
It uses real practice/slap actions and the authoritative host replay, saves
healthy/front/contact injury views, then sends a real recovery emote.
Assertions require a hit and nonzero regional injury, identity propagation
and no browser errors. Native strong-jaw/cheek fixture studies are separate
authored accumulator scenarios, not recordings of additional live strikes.
The real replay trajectory has one severity; extreme accumulated damage is
checked with those fixtures rather than presented as a second browser hit.
