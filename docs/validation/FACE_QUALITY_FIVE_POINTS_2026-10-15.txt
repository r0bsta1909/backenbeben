BACKENBEBEN — FIVE-POINT FACE ART PASS — 2026-10-15

Request
Improve the five identified visual weaknesses in the actual game model:
mouth/chin, eyes/expression, proportions, comic shading, hair/beards.
The user's supplied XIII illustration remains the style target. This pass
uses native model/material work, not a generated or retouched beauty render.
Human art acceptance remains open.

1. Mouth and chin
The v35 oral patch was connected but left a horseshoe-shaped shoulder in
neutral gray material. A continuous muzzle depth field now extends across
the welded patch into the surrounding face. Upper/lower lip rolls, philtrum,
sub-labial transition and chin use separate restrained volumes in this field.
The oral rim rest depth and shared CPU/GPU jaw binding are retained.
The lower-jaw corners are narrower and the central chin transition shorter.
A later attempt to fair the mouth and cheeks together recreated a corner
groove. It was rejected; cheek fairing now excludes the entire oral area.
The shader's oral normal replacement strength falls from .78 to .18: the
gray-model form, rather than a large normal override, carries most volume.

2. Eyes and expression
Upper rest lids are lifted locally without moving globe/iris anchors.
Brows are thinner and sit closer to skin; the neutral inner brow is relaxed.
Focus now coordinates stronger upper-lid tension, lower-lid lift and inner
brow descent. Full blink still suppresses additive focus on the face.
Grin now lifts both mouth corners with restrained asymmetry and adds cheek
projection and lower-lid movement. The earlier expression mainly moved one
corner. These are authoring/local expressions; automatic combat focus and
a persisted expression selector are not claimed by this change.

3. Proportions
Reduced lower-jaw breadth and rounded the mandibular/chin transition.
The upper vault is shortened with one smooth coordinate map. All meshes
and all identity/expression shapes receive that map, including each hair
option; the eye anchors below the forehead remain in place.
All six identities still derive from a common skull/rig.

4. Comic rendering
Reduced the frontal ink guide's fine creases and brush contrast, especially
the lower face, where they conflicted with modeled features and stubble.
Muted the previously saturated blue facial shadow and broadened the light
transition; actual hand/nose shadow attenuation and the damage layers remain.
Finer hair contours and less repetitive internal strokes reduce the drawn
outline around every individual lock. No new bitmap/AI image was generated.
The existing face_ink_v4.png is retained. The result is still a simplified
3D comic interpretation, not a reproduction of the supplied illustration.

5. Hair and beard
The hero haircut uses broader, flatter locks, with roots AND tips embedded
in the scalp mass; a previous nonzero lift at tips made detached-looking ends.
Stubble density is spatially varied, fades at the cheek line and has less
uniform overall darkness. Beard color remains independent of hair color.
The full beard and goatee were rebuilt as densely sampled face-conforming
patches. The former full beard included neck faces and created an apron.
New cheek/lip/neck boundaries are clipped against the actual contours, rather
than accepting whole grid cells and leaving a visible staircase.
Moustaches use cubic centerlines and flatter cross sections, with smaller
volumes. The unusual curled moustache and pointed tuft remain selectable.
The other hair silhouettes remain their established designs; this pass
does not claim six newly sculpted production-quality haircuts.

Contact contract and fixture changes
Character version is now 10 in the host, join message and six exact exported
contact profiles. Old version 9 is rejected, not silently used with new skin.
The sculpt changes actual palm contact. The old v6/v9 fixed pose at -18deg
is now a glance. Measurements on v10 found -15/-12/-9deg flat, -18/-6deg
glancing, -24deg fingertips. Contact classification/coverage thresholds and
the physics implementation were NOT relaxed to pass the suite.
The class fixture now uses -15deg for flat and -18deg for glance. Practice
guidance fixtures use -18/-6deg to preserve actual opposite wheel directions.
Gesture protocol 3 and damage schema 1 remain unchanged.

Limits
Remaining art limitations include the blocky side planes of some identities,
simplified ears and extreme mouth cavities, some coarse hair variants and
residual atlas/geometry lines near the eyes. No final human style approval.
Arena/table/Hoenhorst and character-v3.blend are unchanged. No new release ZIP;
the earlier V14 download is not updated by a source push.
