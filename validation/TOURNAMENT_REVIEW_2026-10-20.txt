TOURNAMENT REVIEW / 2026-10-20
Baseline: a900e2f, automatic LAN tournament for 2..20 players.
User requested review and continued autonomous corrections.

REPRODUCED AND FIXED
1. KO/DQ winner was only committed after replay, so leaving during impact,
   replay or a suspended terminal strike could change it into a forfeit.
   freeze_tournament_result commits the already authoritative outcome before
   withdrawal/expiry. An unrelated room's replay is not shortened. A participant
   leaving after a semifinal still withdraws from their next fight normally.
2. Two clients expiring in the same tick were withdrawn one after another.
   The first withdrawal could award a final to another already-expired client.
   A common expiry batch now marks all affected participants before settlement.
   No remaining players -> cancellation without an invented champion. A real
   KO already determined before the disconnect remains a valid result.
3. Global FIFO replay eviction could remove a paused room's current clip when
   parallel matches created enough traffic. Current room replay IDs are pinned;
   oldest unused clips are removed first, normally retaining a 32-entry cache.
   If >32 rooms genuinely need distinct clips, active clips are retained.
4. A solver exception left a tournament room permanently disconnected, with
   nobody eligible for the next round. Technical failures now explicitly cancel
   the event without awarding a winner; all affected matches end and release
   clients to the bracket. Clear reason shown; unplayed cards say Abgebrochen.
5. Terminal paused rooms retained their old suspended phase. A reconnect during
   the result window could reopen a decided/cancelled match. Terminal transitions
   discard suspension/brace offset; socket resume only restores disconnected rooms.

Also: duplicate start after departed participants produces a clear notice rather
than an internal missing-client key. Async admin/console broadcasts use snapshots
of room/tournament collections to tolerate membership changes during sends.
No art, arena, table, Hoenhorst, combat formulas or damage schemas changed.
