COMIC FACE DEBRIS AND BLOOD / 2026-10-18

User requested freely breaking bone fragments, knocked-out flying teeth and
blood simulation. Implemented as bounded, replayable comic particle/debris
physics, integrated with the existing authoritative injury model.

HOST / server/face_effects.py
build_face_effects compares the real before/after damage snapshots. Cosmetic
events never cause damage. Misses, fouls and unchanged hit counts emit nothing.
Crossing tooth health.75 sheds an enamel chip; crossing.04 detaches the crown.
All16 crowns on all6 identities use their exported dental positions. Detached
crowns retain the selected identity and the damage they had before loss.
Crossing bone fracture.65 with cut>.2 emits three small angular bone fragments
at that region's projected skin surface. Severe wounds show a jagged exposed
fracture patch; this does not replace the anatomical rig with fractured meshes.
Fresh bleeding produces a short staggered spray. An existing open wound can
also shed droplets when struck after its bleeding value saturates; distant
wounds cannot emit from an unrelated hit. Fresh bleeding deltas are recorded
separately for skin streak growth, preserving older wound appearance.
Events store birth times, positions, velocities, sizes and angular velocities.
Head contact rotation/offset and pre-existing lower-jaw fracture are included.
Hard limit96 events per hit. The same clip owns the same events for both clients.
The host adds face_effects.version1 to its replay; character14/gesture3/damage1
contracts remain unchanged. Older clips without effects render safely.

RENDERING / game/face_effects.gd
No wall-clock random emitters. Fixed120Hz tracks, gravity39.24 game units/s^2
(4 units/metre), drag, solid bounce/friction, spin and settling are sampled at
the recorded replay time. Sphere/crown proxies collide with an elliptical
tabletop and the actual mat bounds; the lower surrounding floor is a plane.
Collider bounds come from the approved stage meshes without changing them.
Blood droplets stretch along velocity, land, and expand into irregular dark-red
surface splashes. Skin streaks grow downwards after contact. Permanent injury
values and new bleeding are combined without erasing older blood or tinting
healthy reset skin with a stale transient channel.
Flying teeth use the actual imported crown mesh with identity/chip deformations,
recentered and detached from the rig. The original tooth hides at emission.
Reverse seeking restores its visibility, prior chip shape and material health.
First-person-to-replay actor changes rebuild current trajectories in the new
model's coordinates without archiving/spawning a second copy of the same burst.
At the next hit, settled deposits persist: at most48 blood stains and32 solid
fragments. Oldest deposits are removed at the limit. Lobby/rematch reset frees
effect nodes and cached crown meshes. The former nine random red cubes removed.

LIMITS
This is comic debris and particle blood, not a volumetric fluid solver, dynamic
whole-skeleton fracture, or medically validated injury simulation. Bone pieces
are authored procedural shards; skin fracture exposure is a shader patch.
Collision proxies cover stage surfaces, not other moving characters/clothing.
Single-hardware render checks do not establish general60FPS, human art/gameplay
acceptance or second-PC LAN acceptance. Arena/table/Hoenhorst assets untouched.
Restart the host and reload clients to use the new replay/render code together.
