CHARACTER RENDER PERFORMANCE / 19.10.2026

PROBLEM AND CHANGE
Six character instances contain 27 small cloth/hand/collar ink meshes each.
Their separate draws multiply across lights and shadow passes. The new
character_ink.gd coalesces only identical material, parent, local transform,
skin resource, skeleton path, layers, shadow mode and surface format. It copies
all vertex streams and offsets indices, retaining the actual bone weights.
Source nodes remain available, hidden and excluded from appearance updates.
No face, teeth, hair or beard geometry is merged. No LOD, texture reduction,
shadow removal, arena/table/Hoenhorst change, or character-version change.

Unchanged appearance input no longer resends material and morph values.
The key includes the full fighter data, first-person visibility, emote state
and active impact amount. reset_all invalidates it. Blinks/focus and actual
jaw transforms also avoid identical writes, while recorded motion still owns
the animated pose. Emote rotation resetting retains its prior schedule.

LIMITS AND REPRODUCTION
Two contexts share one PC; no second physical LAN PC or human art acceptance.
Host/contact physics and character source geometry are unchanged. No new ZIP.
Restart older hosts/reload clients after updating the Web build.
Run the local host, set BACKENBEBEN_TEST_PORT to its port, then:
  python tests/render_performance.py --clients 2 --output logs/render-check.json
  Godot --path game --script ../tests/character_ink.gd
The source requires a fresh Web export before benchmarking.
Raw timings, screenshots and match records remain ignored under logs/.
Human visual/gameplay acceptance and universal performance remain open.
