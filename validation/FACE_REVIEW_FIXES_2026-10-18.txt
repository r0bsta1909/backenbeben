FACE REVIEW FIXES / 2026-10-18

Scope: four confirmed findings from the face review, not a claim that the whole
game is free of defects. Comic style, arena, table and Hoenhorst unchanged.

AUTHORITATIVE DENTAL DAMAGE
The previous two fixed index lists could damage only eight of sixteen teeth.
The builder now exports the actual sixteen crown centroids for all six heads in
game/assets/character_dental.json. The host projects authoritative footprint
samples onto the selected dental arc and accumulates deterministic per-tooth
loads with local falloff. Upper/lower rows and opposite sides remain distinct.
Existing snapshots without tooth_loads preserve their previous tooth health.
Damage schema1 remains additive; character asset/protocol version is now14.

BROW ATTACHMENT
Replace expression-independent blink correctives with skin-projected poses for
six identities, two focus endpoints, two alert endpoints and five blink stages.
The authoring samples are factored into48 exported poses: quadratic closure,
three focus corrections, alert and focus/alert interaction per identity. Runtime
combines at most eight poses; cached bindings avoid scanning the full bank every frame.
Ordinary identity keys stay separate.
Brows now use the same tissue shader deformation as facial skin, including
regional swelling. apply_fighter refreshes expression pose immediately, including
creator/mirror updates before the next contact frame.

ALERT EMOTE
Remove the raw upward Brow node translation from emote1 (Noch wach). An authored
skin alert shape and matching brow poses raise the expression along the forehead.
Grin/kiss behavior remains emote0/1 grin.8, emote2 kiss.85; alert.8 only emote1.
The appearance test verifies alert activation/reset and unchanged brow origins.

UV CONTRACT
The old test incorrectly decoded UV2.V as rest-Z. It now verifies glTF inverted
jaw ownership (1-UV2.V), finite paint coordinates, front/side paint masks, and all
six imported identity surfaces against actual host contact profiles. Coincident
seam vertices are matched without relying on imported vertex ordering.
