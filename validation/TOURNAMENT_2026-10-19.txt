LAN TOURNAMENT / 2026-10-19

SCOPE
User authorized 2-20 player automatic single-elimination tournaments, replacing
the earlier deferral. No ElevenLabs, new advertisements or visual asset changes.
Six-character lobby code, owner start, all participant counts, random bracket,
first-round byes, parallel round matches, automatic next rounds, one champion.
Fresh wounds/stun/load/points every fight; settings frozen for the tournament.
Ties: up to three extra complete pairs, then a clearly labelled fair predrawn lot.
Normal duels retain draws and rematches. Exiting means withdrawal; reconnect
grace is 30 seconds. Results remain visible for six seconds. No disk persistence.
Public player IDs are separated from private 256-bit resume credentials.
Private credentials are only returned to their client in hello.resume.

LIMITS
All automated clients ran on one Windows PC with the local host. No test on
20 physical LAN PCs, no 20-client physics/render performance claim, no human
controls, fairness/balance or art acceptance. Record-driven browser inputs do
not establish good human controls. Refreshing a cold 3D client can take longer
than a warm socket reconnect; grace remains a fixed 30 seconds.
Host reset deliberately cancels without a champion; host restart loses brackets.
Existing release v0.14 predates this feature; source and local Web build updated.
